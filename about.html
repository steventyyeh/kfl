About

Name: Porf Jou   
Province Origin: Chekiang, China.   
Age: 60 years old.

Shifu Jou strated to learn nothern Chinese martial art at the age of 6.
His Shifu was Wang Song-tyng, who was a well-known master in Ching Dao Central Chinese Martial art Institute.
Master Wang specialized in Chinese Kung-fu to the degree which few people could attain to.
In that case, he won a title ----- "Kung-fu King Kong". 
Shifu Jou was his last disciple.
Shifu Jou followed his master's will, continued the task of promoting Chinese Kung-fu.
His students are all over the US, Canada and Australia.
Kung-fu Loung has operated for one year and acguired a lot of results.
Shifu Jou's ambition is to provide an opportunity to every one who is willing to learn Kung-fu.
The video tapes of Kung-fu Loung will help you to learn Kung-fu for yourself.

With one more man learning Kung-fu, the spreading power will be much more stronger.
There are 300 Kinds of Chyuan for your choice.
By this way, Chinese Kung-fu can hand down from generation to generation.
